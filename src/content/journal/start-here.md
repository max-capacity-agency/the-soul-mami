I started journaling when I was 12 years old. I've kept all my journals for the last 28 years. Stacks of them. The kind with coffee rings on the covers and sentences that trail off mid-thought because the feeling moved faster than the pen. Teenage stickers here. Highlighted downloads there.

Journaling has been the one faithful tool that has helped me "see" myself on paper. All the selves. The me after a break-up. The me full of doubt. The dreamer me, doer me, lonely me. The me that died and that me that rebirthed. Again and again.

This section of the site is me cracking a few of those notebooks open.

This is not a blog so you won't find "5 tips for optimal morning routines." You will see my soul in writing, walking through the human experience. The grief and the goofiness, the doubt spirals and the little wins, the days I remember who I am and the days I forget.

I'm sharing it because I think we heal out loud, in company. And because maybe something in my pages helps you open yours.

Take your time here. The archives aren't going anywhere.
