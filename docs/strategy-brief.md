# The Soul Mami — Website Strategy Brief

**Client:** Eliana Reyes · thesoulmami.com
**Prepared by:** Max Capacity Agency
**Date:** September 2026

---

## 1. Client snapshot

Eliana Reyes is a mentor, facilitator, author, TEDx speaker, and independent music artist. She has facilitated 200+ circles and workshops, written two books, and releases music on Spotify. Her work lives under one roof: The Soul Mami.

She offers:
- **The Journaling Mastermind** — monthly gathering, first Sunday of every month
- **1:1 Mentoring** — deep personal work sessions
- **Music** — original songs as another doorway into the same work
- **Facilitation, workshops & speaking** — for organizations, retreats, and events (B2B lane)

## 2. Core message

> "I am my own missing piece."

Everything The Soul Mami does is about **self-belonging**: being in a healthy, loving marriage with yourself — mind, body, and soul. People are already complete; the work is removing the layers of identities that aren't them.

**The three pillars** (Eliana's own framework — these structure the whole site):
1. **Know yourself**
2. **Be in relationship with yourself**
3. **Live, express, and create as yourself**

## 3. Target audience

People stuck in loops of false identities. Smart, intelligent, capable — but unable to express that self in the outside world. They want to know themselves better, build self-trust, and live authentically.

Three overlapping faces of the same person:
- **Women in self-reconnection** — years of performing roles (career, motherhood, relationships), now asking "who am I underneath all this?"
- **Creatives & seekers** — artists, writers, spiritually curious people hungry for tools, community, and permission to express
- **Burnt-out professionals** — did everything "right," feel empty, need to come home to themselves

**Pains:** performing an identity that isn't theirs; self-doubt despite competence; disconnection from their own inner voice; feeling unseen; exhaustion from self-abandonment.

**Desires:** self-trust; authentic expression; feeling alive; belonging to themselves first; a guide who is a real human, not a guru.

## 4. Value proposition & transformation

**Before:** looping in borrowed identities, second-guessing yourself, brilliance locked inside.
**After:** knowing your soul, trusting yourself, expressing what only you came here to express.

One line: *Eliana helps you remember you are already complete — then teaches you the tools to live like it.*

## 5. USPs / differentiation (by contrast)

- **Not a guru.** No pedestal, no "I have all the answers." She's a life-liver walking beside you — "here's someone that's gone through it."
- **Not a content machine.** No professional blog; real journal entries from a real human going through real human experiences.
- **Not one modality.** Journaling, music, mentoring, circles — multiple doorways into the same room: you.
- **Ancestral grounding.** A lineage of beekeepers, farmers, and storytellers — respect for cycles, organic intelligence, and the wisdom that lives within and around us. (This also drives the visual identity: honey, amber, earth.)
- **Emotional alchemist.** Give her any emotion; she can transmute it and find the lesson. Comfortable where others flinch.

## 7. Traffic & story strategy

**Warm-dominant:** existing email list, Instagram, word of mouth, Spotify listeners, TEDx viewers. Visitors mostly already know Eliana.

Implications: compressed need-building (one tight section, not two), fast route to the three pillars and offers, heavy investment in **depth content** (journal, music, goodies) because the site's job is to make warm visitors *stay, return, and subscribe* — the "three drawers you open and get lost in" experience Eliana described.

## 8. Voice & tone

Warm, real, soulful, funny as hell. Like talking to a dope, wise friend. "LFG" energy without profanity. First person, conversational rhythm, Spanglish touches welcome (Hola, mami). Never guru-speak, never corporate-wellness sanitized.

## 9. Sitemap

- **Home** — the full narrative arc; routes visitors into the garden (email) and the three drawers
- **About** — lineage story, why she's uniquely positioned, TEDx/books/200+ circles as narrative not resume
- **Work With Me** — overview of the three drawers + facilitation/speaking lane
  - **Journaling** — The Journaling Mastermind detail page
  - **Mentoring** — 1:1 detail page
  - **Music** — songs, story behind them, Spotify embeds
- **Journal** — Eliana's personal entries (Markdown collection; she adds entries over time; the archive/return-visit engine)
- **Goodies** — free tools for self-connection (downloads, prompts, resources)
- **Let's Talk** — contact; low-friction, includes facilitation/speaking inquiries

## 10. Conversion strategy

- **Primary CTA: "Join my Garden"** — email list signup, present in the header, homepage hero, and a global CTA band on every page. The garden metaphor (bees, honey, seeds) carries the brand.
- **Secondary CTAs per pillar:** join the mastermind, book a mentoring conversation, listen on Spotify.
- **B2B CTA:** Let's Talk for facilitation/speaking.
- **Soft exit:** Goodies as the no-commitment entry point.
- Anti-salesy language throughout: "No pitch. No pressure. Just a conversation."

## 11. Design direction

Earthy warm with honey: cream/sand foundation, terracotta and clay, **gold/amber/honey accents**, subtle bee and honeycomb motifs honoring the beekeeper lineage. Serif display type with organic character (Fraunces), humanist sans for body (Karla). Generous whitespace — minimalist but deep. Grain and warmth over gloss.
